## About Me:
Hi, I'm Gourav Chandra. I'm a Biomedical Engineering undergraduate at the National Institute of Technology (NIT) Raipur, interested in applying Machine Learning and Artificial Intelligence to healthcare problems.<br>My primary interests lie in Medical Imaging, Biomedical Signal Processing, and Healthcare AI. I'm currently building a strong foundation in mathematics, machine learning, and programming while exploring how data-driven methods can improve diagnosis, monitoring, and clinical decision-making.<br>Over time, I hope to contribute to research at the intersection of AI and Healthcare and pursue graduate studies in this area.<br><br>I'm currently working on: Projects in AI for healthcare, biomedical signal processing, and software solutions with real-world impact.<br>I'm currently learning: Machine learning, deep learning, EEG signal processing, and the mathematics behind modern AI.<br>I'm looking to collaborate on: Research projects and open-source work in AI, healthcare, medical imaging, and biomedical signal processing.<br>I'm looking for help with: Opportunities to learn from researchers, contribute to AI research, and gain hands-on experience in healthcare AI.<br>Ask me about: Python, machine learning, biomedical signal processing, hackathons, or anything related to AI in healthcare.<br>Fun fact: Most of my projects revolve around solving real-world problems—from rural sanitation and healthcare triage to EEG signal analysis.<br>
